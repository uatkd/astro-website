The University of Alberta Taekwondo Club is one of the biggest clubs on campus. We are proud to have a large community that is welcoming and includes athletes of all skill and fitness levels. Our club has a wide range of members with varying degrees of experience and proficiency, from black belts to white belts to people who are not yet familiar with the belt system. We are a great fit for anyone who wants to improve their level of fitness or be involved in martial arts. Any interested athletes are encouraged to try our club out.
